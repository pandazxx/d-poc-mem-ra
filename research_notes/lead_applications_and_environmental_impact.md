# Lead Applications and Environmental Impact Research Notes
## Key Statistics
- The global lead market size was valued at $15.4 billion in 2020 and is expected to reach $24.8 billion by 2027, growing at a CAGR of 5.5% during the forecast period. (Source: Grand View Research)
- The use of lead in batteries accounts for around 85% of global lead consumption. (Source: International Lead Association)
- Lead is used in radiation shielding, accounting for around 5% of global lead consumption. (Source: International Lead Association)
- The environmental impact of lead is a significant concern, with lead pollution affecting air, water, and soil quality. (Source: Environmental Protection Agency)
- The recycling rate of lead-acid batteries is around 99% in the United States, making it one of the most recycled materials in the country. (Source: Environmental Protection Agency)
