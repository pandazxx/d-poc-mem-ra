# Lead Recycling Processes
## Key Statistics
* 70% of lead is recycled from spent lead-acid batteries (Source: EPA)
* The lead recycling industry is projected to grow at a CAGR of 5.5% from 2023 to 2030 (Source: Grand View Research)
* The global lead market size was valued at $15.4 billion in 2022 and is expected to reach $24.6 billion by 2030 (Source: MarketsandMarkets)

## Trends & Projections
* The increasing demand for lead-acid batteries in the automotive and renewable energy sectors is driving the growth of the lead recycling industry
* Advances in recycling technologies and equipment are improving the efficiency and effectiveness of lead recycling processes
* The lead recycling industry is expected to play a critical role in reducing lead waste and pollution in the coming years
